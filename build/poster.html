TRUMP: ‘I PERSUADED THE BOARD’ — HUANG BACK AT NVIDIA

The board reversed itself eleven days after he stepped down. The shares jumped more than 30%, all three New York indices soared, and Washington is now arguing over what was promised to whom — and whether chips were part of the bargain.

Nvidia's board reinstated Jensen Huang as chief executive eleven days after he stepped down, and the stock jumped more than 30% as all three main New York indices soared. President Trump said his intervention “was very important” and that he had “persuaded the board”. Democrats called it a security exemption and demanded a full investigation into chip exports to China. JD Vance had lobbied the board over the weekend.

COP31 ENDS WITH NO DEAL, A FIRST IN 17 YEARS

COP31 in Antalya ended with no agreement after two weeks, the first failure of a UN climate summit in 17 years. Oil producers resisted; Antonio Guterres blamed “powerful fossil fuel interests”.
